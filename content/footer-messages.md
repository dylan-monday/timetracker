# M+P Time Tracker — Footer Messages

## Usage Notes

These replace the current rotating quotes system. They should:
- Appear one at a time in the footer area
- Rotate on page load or at a long interval (not rapidly)
- All feel like they come from the same voice — the product's voice
- Never feel like a motivational poster or corporate encouragement
- Reinforce the product philosophy: this is about awareness and agency, not surveillance

Mix in 10-15 real quotes from creative thinkers (marked with attribution below). The rest are product-voice statements with no attribution — they're the app talking.
